# Week 9 – Business Trend Analysis using Line Graph in Tableau

## Objective

To analyze changes in business performance over time using a Line Graph in Tableau and identify important trends, peaks, drops, and performance patterns.

## Problem Statement

A business-related dataset was analyzed using Tableau Public to understand employee sales performance and business trends. A Line Graph was created to visualize changes in sales over time along with additional visualizations for better business analysis.

## Dataset

**Dataset:** Employee Sales Performance Dataset


## Dashboard

**Dashboard Name:** Employee Sales Performance Analysis Dashboard

The dashboard combines multiple visualizations to analyze employee sales performance and business trends.

## Visualizations Created

### 1. Sales Trend – Line Graph

The Line Graph shows how sales change over time and helps identify:

- Highest sales period
- Lowest sales period
- Increasing trends
- Decreasing trends
- Sudden peaks and drops

### 2. Sales by Employee

A chart is used to compare the sales performance of different employees.

### 3. Sales by Department

A visualization is used to analyze sales contribution across different departments.

### 4. Sales Performance Analysis

An additional visualization is used to understand overall employee and business performance.

## Interactive Filter

An interactive filter is added to the dashboard to allow users to analyze the data based on the selected category.

## Business Insights

1. The Line Graph helps identify changes in sales performance over time.
2. The highest sales period can be identified from the sales trend.
3. The lowest sales period can be identified from the Line Graph.
4. Peaks and sudden drops in sales can be observed and analyzed.
5. Employee and department-level visualizations help compare business performance.

## Business Recommendations

1. Focus on periods and departments showing strong sales performance.
2. Analyze low-performing periods and employees to identify areas for improvement.

## Conclusion

The Employee Sales Performance Analysis Dashboard provides a clear visual representation of business performance. The Line Graph helps understand sales trends over time, while the additional visualizations provide insights into employee and department-level performance.
